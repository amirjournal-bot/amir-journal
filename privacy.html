Amir Journal Privacy Policy

Data we collect

Your email address for sign-up and sign-in; if you use Google sign-in, only the email, name and profile picture of your Google account. Also the content you enter in the app: strategy rules, trades, notes and screenshots.

How we use it

Only to sign you in and show your own data in the app. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Storage

Data is stored on the Supabase cloud service; each user can access only their own data. The app administrator can see the list of accounts and aggregate usage for support.

Deletion

To delete your account and all your data, email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.
